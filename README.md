# Hi, I'm Tom

I build **[Tīrtha](https://tirtha.ai)**, a verification router for coding agents, at **Spanda Works**, a small AI company in Rock Hill, South Carolina.

Tīrtha checks an AI's answer before it hands it to you. Tool calls are checked against your schema. Code runs against your tests in a sandbox, and you only get code that passes. Every response says whether it was verified, and when there was nothing to check against, it says that too.

Keep your client, your prompts and your tools. Change one line, your base URL. **[Free to test](https://tirtha.ai/pricing)**: 100 requests a month, no card. After that, each request is billed at the level that served it.

### Where I came from

- **Director of IT, Savoy ISD, Texas.** Ran a school district's technology on a school budget and taught every day. In 2006 our UIL Computer Science team won the Texas state championship.
- **Sixteen years at Vanguard in compliance oversight.** It teaches one habit for life: do not trust a claim until you can check it.
- **Now:** building that habit into the tools AI agents use.
